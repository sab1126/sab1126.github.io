About Me

I am currently a Master's student in Computer Science at National Taiwan University (NTU).

Under the guidance of Prof. Jyh-Shing Roger Jang in the MIR Lab, my research focuses on AI applications in FinTech, particularly leveraging large language models (LLMs) for quantitative trading.

I am also involved in a collaborative project with E.SUN Financial Holding, assisting in applying LLM-based methods to financial data analysis and evaluating their effectiveness.
